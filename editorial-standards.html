Editorial standards

This site makes practical claims about housing, benefits, healthcare, compensation, and support routes. Those claims can matter at a council desk, a GP surgery, or during a benefits call. They must be checkable.

How claims are sourced

Legal, benefits, housing, health, pension, and scheme-status claims should be traced to primary sources wherever possible: legislation.gov.uk, gov.uk, mygov.scot, Social Security Scotland, NHS Scotland or named official programme pages.

Charity pages can be used for what that charity says it provides. News articles and charity blogs are not treated as proof for legal rights or statutory deadlines.

Review cadence

Volatile claims — phone numbers, deadlines, scheme status, legislation references, and named programmes — should carry a review date in the source register. The footer review date should only be updated after the verification pass is complete.

Corrections

If you know the system and spot an error, please report it. Include the page or wording, what you believe is wrong, and the source you are relying on.

Correction route: contact page. If the correction concerns an urgent legal, benefits, housing, or health claim, mark it as urgent in the message.

Verification status

A repository citation register has been added for the current verification pass. Claims that cannot be supported by a primary source must be resolved before the site presents them as certain rights.
