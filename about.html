About this site

Leaving the Forces in Scotland is an independent civic guide for service leavers, veterans, and families in Scotland. It exists to make the first practical steps around housing, benefits, health, money, and support easier to understand.

Who runs it

The site is operated by AI Scotland Productions as part of a portfolio of practical public-interest tools. It is not affiliated with the Ministry of Defence, the Scottish Government, NHS Scotland, Social Security Scotland, or any organisation listed in the guide.

Funding

This site has no ads, no affiliate links, no sponsorship, no lead generation, and no paid placement. Listed services are included because they appear relevant to veterans and families in Scotland, not because anyone has paid to be included.

What it is and is not

This is informational help only. It is not legal, financial, medical, benefits, or housing advice. Always verify current entitlements and deadlines with the relevant agency or a qualified adviser before relying on them.
